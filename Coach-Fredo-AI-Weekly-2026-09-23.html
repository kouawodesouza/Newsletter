This week the frontier went on sale. Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and OpenAI's GPT-6 Sol and Luna landed within about 90 minutes of each other on Tuesday, both promising top-tier work at a fraction of last quarter's price. For small businesses, the question is no longer “can we afford AI that's good enough?” — it's “which jobs are we still doing by hand?”

— Coach Fredo

THIS WEEK AT A GLANCE

  • Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5.5 — about 40% cheaper than Opus 5 on typical workloads (The Neuron)
  • OpenAI answers with GPT-6 Sol ($2/$10 per million tokens) and budget model Luna ($0.10/$0.50) (TechCrunch)
  • Gemini in Google Workspace now connects to QuickBooks, HubSpot, Mailchimp, Salesforce and more via MCP (Google Workspace Updates)
  • Manus seeks $500M at a $4B valuation after its forced split from Meta (TechCrunch)
  • China's internet regulator probes DeepSeek and Moonshot over data routed through Claude (Seoul Economic Daily)
  • xAI's Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0 doubles transcription accuracy at the same price (xAI)
THE BIG SIX

ChatGPT / OpenAI

On September 22, OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna, cheaper siblings of its flagship GPT-6 Astra. Sol is priced at $2 / $10 per million input/output tokens — half the price of the GPT-5.6 tier — while Luna comes in at just $0.10 / $0.50. OpenAI also confirmed that GPT-5.5 retires from ChatGPT and Codex on October 14, 2026.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: These price cuts flow into every app built on OpenAI's API, so expect cheaper, faster AI features in tools you already pay for — and check any saved workflows or custom GPTs that rely on GPT-5.5 before October 14.
Source: TechCrunch · OpenAI release notes (Releasebot)

Gemini / Google

Google announced that Gemini in Google Workspace can now connect directly to seven business apps — Asana, Atlassian Rovo, HubSpot, Intuit Mailchimp, Intuit QuickBooks, monday.com and Salesforce — using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Once an admin enables the connectors, users can pull information from those systems into the Gemini side panel in Docs, Sheets, Slides and Google Chat without downloading or copy-pasting.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you run your business on Google Workspace plus QuickBooks or HubSpot, you can soon ask Gemini about invoices or your sales pipeline right inside a Doc — but an admin has to switch it on, so decide who gets access first.
Source: Google Workspace Updates

Claude / Anthropic

Anthropic released Claude Opus 5.5 on September 22. It's priced at $4 / $20 per million tokens, and Anthropic says typical workloads cost about 40% less than Opus 5 because the model uses fewer tokens; early long-running tests included a 680,000-line code migration in under a day. Days earlier, Anthropic said Claude now leads about 26% of its own model research and development under human supervision.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Top-tier quality at a lower price makes it more affordable to hand off longer, multi-step jobs — proposals, reports, cleaning up messy spreadsheets — instead of just quick questions.
Source: Anthropic · U.S. News / Reuters

Manus

Manus is seeking about $500 million at a $4 billion valuation — roughly double its previous value — in its first fundraise since Beijing forced the unwinding of its $2 billion acquisition by Meta. Reported potential investors include IDG Capital, Boyu Capital and battery maker CATL, and the company may restructure ahead of a possible Hong Kong IPO.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Manus is independent again and well-funded, so its general-purpose agent should keep improving — but the ownership whiplash is a good reminder to keep your own copies of any work you run through agent platforms.
Source: TechCrunch

Microsoft / Copilot

A quieter, incremental week. In Teams, the Facilitator meeting agent can now run a Copilot web search when a factual question goes unanswered and post the answer in the meeting chat. In Excel, Copilot adds finance-focused skills, with Plan with Copilot, workbook rules and Copilot attribution in Show Changes now generally available for Microsoft 365 Copilot customers.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you pay for Microsoft 365 Copilot, the new Excel attribution means you can see exactly which changes the AI made to a budget or forecast — a simple but important step toward trusting AI with your numbers.
Source: UC Today · Microsoft Copilot updates (Releasebot)

Grok / xAI

On September 18, xAI released Grok Voice Transcribe 2.0, a speech-to-text model with multilingual transcription, word timestamps, speaker separation (diarization) and multichannel support. xAI says it is twice as accurate as version 1.0 at the same price, and that Grok Voice already handles tens of thousands of customer-support calls a day. Grok 5 is still in training.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Accurate, affordable transcription is the building block for call summaries, meeting notes and voice agents — worth a small test if your business lives on the phone.
Source: xAI News
INTERNATIONAL

🇫🇷 France

On September 22, President Macron launched the roadmap of the Coalition for Children's Rights and Protection in the Age of AI, with the first voluntary commitments due at the Paris Peace Forum on November 10–11, 2026. Macron framed protecting children online as a public-health priority, extending the same approach France took when it banned social media for under-15s.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you build or sell chatbots, AI tools or learning content that young people might use, expectations around child safety in Europe are rising — build in age-appropriate defaults now rather than retrofitting later.
Source: Élysée

🇪🇺 European Union

The EU AI Board met on September 17 to advance enforcement coordination across member states, but added no new AI Act obligations or compliance deadlines. That means the rules already in force — including the transparency duties for AI systems and AI-generated content that took effect in August — remain the ones businesses need to follow today.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: No new deadline is not a reprieve: if you sell into Europe, make sure customers know when they're talking to a chatbot and label AI-generated content.
Source: Quasa · Al Jazeera

🇨🇳 China

China's Cyberspace Administration sent officials to DeepSeek and Moonshot AI to investigate whether routing user requests through Anthropic's Claude broke China's data security law, and Chinese AI stocks fell on the news. Separately, Alibaba unveiled a new domestic AI chip and a target of 20 gigawatts of data-center capacity by 2032.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Data sovereignty is now being enforced on both sides of the Pacific — ask every AI vendor you use where your data is processed and which underlying models sit behind their product.
Source: Seoul Economic Daily · Fortune

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY

Top-tier AI just got cheaper again, so re-run the math on one task you once ruled out as “too expensive to automate” — a weekly report, proposal drafts, or call summaries. And before you connect AI to your books or CRM (Gemini now does this with QuickBooks and HubSpot), spend ten minutes checking who can see what and where the data goes. Cheap AI is great; cheap AI with sloppy permissions is not.
