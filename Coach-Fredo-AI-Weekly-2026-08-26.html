This week's theme: the free-for-all is ending. OpenAI reinstated usage caps on ChatGPT Work and Codex, DeepSeek added peak-hour pricing, and the EU is now actively enforcing AI Act fines — even as Claude and Gemini rolled out new always-on conveniences. The tools keep getting smarter and more persistent, but they're also asking us to pay closer attention to what we use, when, and under what rules.

— Coach Fredo

THIS WEEK AT A GLANCE

  • OpenAI reinstated a 5-hour usage cap on ChatGPT Work and Codex for Plus subscribers starting Aug. 25, after weeks with no limit (OpenAI Help Center)
  • Google launched "Ask Gemini" inside Google Chat on Aug. 26, turning team chat into an AI command line (Google Workspace Updates)
  • Anthropic gave Claude persistent memory across Claude.ai and Cowork, with full user control to view or delete it (Anthropic)
  • Manus AI resumed independent operations this week after Meta's $2B acquisition was unwound by Chinese regulators (Bloomberg)
  • xAI's Grok hit a "rare temporary generation glitch" this week that served some users nonsensical text (Gizmodo)
  • DeepSeek introduced peak/off-peak pricing this month, doubling costs for 7 daytime hours but still undercutting Western rivals (Bloomberg)
THE BIG SIX

ChatGPT / OpenAI

OpenAI reinstated a 5-hour usage cap on Codex and ChatGPT Work for Plus subscribers starting August 25, after removing the limit for several weeks — the same week OpenAI retired the o3 model from ChatGPT (Aug. 26) following a 90-day sunset period. GPT-5.6 Luna is also rolling out as the default model for Free and Go users, with unlimited basic chats and a new "Think" button for harder questions.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you lean on Plus for coding or research marathons, plan around the reinstated cap — free-tier users are actually gaining capability, not losing it.
Source: OpenAI Help Center

Gemini / Google

Google rolled out "Ask Gemini" inside Google Chat on August 26, giving teams a unified command line to summarize threads, draft replies, and pull Workspace context without leaving chat — part of a broader Workspace Intelligence push. The launch follows Gemini crossing 1 billion monthly users this month.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If your team already runs on Google Chat, this adds an AI layer with no new app, login, or subscription to manage.
Source: Google Workspace Updates

Claude / Anthropic

Anthropic announced on August 25 that Claude's memory now works across Claude.ai and Claude Cowork, updating as you chat with options to view, edit, delete, pause, or reset what it remembers. The rollout followed a rockier-than-usual week with two separate service incidents (Aug. 20 and Aug. 24) causing elevated errors on flagship models.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Cross-platform memory means less time re-explaining your business context each session — review the privacy controls before letting it retain client details.
Source: Anthropic

Manus AI

Manus AI resumed independent operations this week, completing the unwind of Meta's $2 billion acquisition after Chinese regulators blocked the deal on national-security grounds. Users had until August 23 to back up data generated during the period of Meta ownership before some of it was deleted to meet regulatory requirements.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you use Manus for agentic research or task automation, confirm your workflows and stored data survived the split before relying on it for anything time-sensitive.
Source: Bloomberg

Microsoft / Copilot & Azure AI

Microsoft's August Microsoft 365 Copilot update — 59 changes spanning Excel, PowerPoint, Word, Chat, and Cowork — includes automatic model choice: Cowork now routes deep research and citation-heavy work to OpenAI's GPT-5.5 and visual tasks like slides and graphics to Anthropic's models, without you picking a model manually. This lands alongside OpenAI formally becoming a data subprocessor inside Microsoft 365 Copilot.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: One less decision for you to make — Copilot is starting to route your work to whichever model handles it best, at no extra cost or complexity.
Source: A Guide to Cloud & AI

Grok / xAI

xAI brought Grok 4.6 — its 500k-context, configurable-reasoning flagship — to Google's enterprise Model Garden on August 22, putting it alongside Google's own models for business customers. The expansion came just as some users reported Grok serving nonsensical text between August 19 and 24, which xAI attributed to a "rare temporary generation glitch."

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Grok is now easier to test through infrastructure you may already use via Google Cloud, but the recent reliability hiccup is a reminder to keep a human check on anything client-facing.
Source: Basenor
INTERNATIONAL

🇫🇷 France — Mistral AI

Mistral and Saudi AI firm HUMAIN announced a strategic collaboration on August 24 spanning AI infrastructure, model development, and regional deployment in Saudi Arabia — Mistral's latest move to fund its buildout through international partnerships. It follows Mistral's early-August launch of "European Compute Units," a structure pooling long-term enterprise commitments to fund sovereign EU compute capacity.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Mistral's push for sovereign, cost-efficient compute could mean more affordable EU-hosted AI options for businesses wary of depending entirely on U.S. cloud providers.
Source: Mistral AI

🇪🇺 European Union — AI Act

The European Commission's AI Office is now actively enforcing the AI Act's transparency rules, which took effect August 2: chatbots must disclose they're AI, deepfakes must be labeled, and AI-generated content needs machine-readable marks, with fines up to €15 million or 3% of global turnover for violators. Over 180 companies have already signed the voluntary Code of Practice to demonstrate compliance.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If your business uses AI chatbots or produces AI-generated content for EU customers, disclosure labeling is no longer optional — check your tools' compliance settings this week.
Source: European Commission

🇨🇳 China — DeepSeek, Alibaba & Moonshot

A Bloomberg analysis published August 18 detailed how DeepSeek, Alibaba's Qwen, and Moonshot's Kimi are matching frontier-model performance at a fraction of U.S. and European pricing. DeepSeek also restructured its own pricing this month with a peak/off-peak split that doubles rates during seven daytime hours — yet it remains far cheaper than Western alternatives.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If cost matters more to you than having the single best model, Chinese open-weight options are worth evaluating for routine tasks — just weigh data-residency and compliance tradeoffs first.
Source: Bloomberg

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY

This week's pattern is limits coming back into fashion — usage caps, pricing tiers, and enforcement deadlines all tightened at once. Spend 15 minutes this week checking which AI tools your business depends on for time-sensitive work, and confirm you know their current usage caps and pricing before you're caught mid-task. And if you serve EU customers with any AI-generated content or chatbot, add a simple "AI-generated" disclosure now — it's cheap insurance against a rule that's no longer theoretical.
