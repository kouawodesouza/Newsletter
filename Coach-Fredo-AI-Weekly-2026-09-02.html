AI is getting both more expensive to access and more deeply embedded in institutions at the same time.

THE BIG SIX

ChatGPT / OpenAI

OpenAI added support for connecting multiple Gmail, Google Calendar, and Google Contacts accounts inside ChatGPT on August 28, letting users check personal and work inboxes or calendars in the same conversation. The move comes as OpenAI also wound down its model-supply contract with Cursor (effective November 12) after Cursor's parent Anysphere was acquired by SpaceX, triggering a change-of-control clause.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you juggle a personal and business Google account, turning on the new multi-account connector is a small change that saves real time every day.
Source: OpenAI Help Center

Gemini / Google

Google will begin retiring Google Assistant on Android, Wear OS, and connected headphones starting September 4, replacing it entirely with Gemini — a transition Google says will take several weeks to reach all devices and can't be reversed once it lands on a given phone. It's the clearest signal yet that Gemini is becoming Google's default assistant everywhere, not just a chat app.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If your business runs on Android devices, expect voice commands and quick tasks to start routing through Gemini automatically in the coming weeks — worth telling your team before it surprises them.
Source: Search Engine Land

Claude / Anthropic

Anthropic confirmed that starting September 14, Claude Code's permanent weekly usage limits rise 25% above the original baseline — but because a temporary +50% promotional boost expires September 13, the net effect for anyone currently on the promo is roughly a 17% cut from what they have today. Anthropic said the pullback reflects difficulty securing enough compute to sustain the elevated limits permanently.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you've built workflows around the temporary boost, budget for about 17% less Claude Code capacity starting September 14 — the headline "25% increase" is measured against an older baseline, not what you're using right now.
Source: BleepingComputer

Manus AI

Manus AI confirmed it has fully completed its separation from Meta and resumed independent operations, more than four months after Chinese regulators blocked Meta's $2 billion acquisition on national-security grounds. The Singapore-based team said the platform isn't shutting down and is now preparing new agent features as a standalone company.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: The ownership drama is resolved — if you paused any Manus-based workflows during the uncertainty, it's a reasonable time to pick them back up.
Source: Tech Startups

Microsoft / Copilot & Azure AI

Microsoft's Copilot "Super App" — merging Copilot Chat, Copilot Cowork, and GitHub Copilot into one experience across consumer and commercial users — began rolling out this week. The same week, six GitHub Copilot models retired, promotional credit allowances dropped 37–44%, and Copilot Studio moved to usage-based "harness" billing, effective September 1.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you or your developers rely on GitHub Copilot, check your plan now — the credit cuts and model retirements landed with little fanfare and could change your monthly costs.
Source: Big Hat Group

Grok / xAI

xAI's Grok for Government joined the Pentagon's GenAI.mil portal on August 31, giving roughly 3 million Defense Department personnel access to a customized Grok build alongside ChatGPT Mil and Google's Gemini for Government — all authorized for sensitive unclassified data. The launch comes weeks after SpaceX's acquisition of xAI was formalized under the combined SpaceXAI name.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Government-grade security validation is a trust signal, even if you'll never touch the defense version — it suggests Grok's security posture is maturing faster than its consumer-facing reputation might suggest.
Source: TechCrunch
INTERNATIONAL

🇫🇷 France — Mistral AI

A quiet week for Mistral: no major dated announcement since last issue. The company remains focused on its previously reported funding talks (roughly €20 billion valuation) and the continued rollout of Work Mode inside Le Chat/Vibe, which lets the agent execute multi-step workflows across connected business tools.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Nothing new to act on this week — but if you haven't tried Le Chat's Work Mode for a real workflow yet, it's worth a test run while things are quiet.
Source: Mistral AI

🇪🇺 European Union — AI Act

OpenAI disclosed on August 27 that a supply-chain attack via a compromised TanStack npm package led to unauthorized access to a limited set of internal source code repositories, though no product or user data was affected. Compliance analysts at IAPP pointed to the incident as a case study for the EU AI Act's deployer obligations — businesses that deploy high-risk AI systems must document their incident response and notify authorities "without undue delay" if a supplier issue poses a risk.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you deploy AI tools that touch EU customer data, this is a reminder to have an actual documented incident-response process for vendor issues, not just a mental plan — regulators are starting to ask for the paperwork.
Source: IAPP

🇨🇳 China — DeepSeek & Alibaba

DeepSeek closed in on a roughly $7.4 billion funding round at a $74 billion valuation this week, hiring investment banks ahead of a targeted 2027 IPO on Shanghai's STAR Market. Days later, Alibaba completed the largest follow-on stock offering in Hong Kong history — about $10.2 billion (HK$80 billion) — with proceeds earmarked entirely for full-stack AI investment and a promised 3-year payback to investors.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Both moves signal Chinese AI labs are betting on years, not quarters, of continued build-out — worth watching if you're evaluating cost-effective open-weight models for the long term.
Source: Tech Startups

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY

This week's lesson is in the fine print: Anthropic's "25% increase" to Claude Code limits is actually a cut from what heavy users have today, and Microsoft quietly cut GitHub Copilot credits by up to 44% the same week. Before you celebrate a headline change to any AI tool your business runs on, check the before-and-after numbers yourself. And if you deploy AI systems for EU customers, start a simple incident-response log now — the AI Act increasingly expects deployers, not just vendors, to show their work.
