The theme this week is the price of 'free.' ChatGPT is adding ads, Google is pulling free users back to its lightest Gemini model, and Microsoft is splitting Copilot into a flat fee plus usage billing. At the same time, Mistral and DeepSeek show that cheaper, capable alternatives keep arriving. Know what you use, know what it costs, and choose on purpose.

— Coach Fredo

THIS WEEK AT A GLANCE

  • OpenAI starts selling visual ads next to ChatGPT image results (U.S. only, later this month) (TechCrunch)
  • Google limits free Gemini users to Flash-Lite starting October 9 (AI Weekly)
  • Anthropic offers eligible startups a free year of Claude Team plus $1,000 in credits (TechCrunch)
  • Microsoft splits Copilot into a flat license plus usage-based billing (ChangePilot)
  • France's Mistral unveils 1-trillion-parameter Mistral Large 4 (TechCrunch)
  • DeepSeek reportedly nears a $12 billion round led by Tencent and CATL (The Next Web)
THE BIG SIX

ChatGPT

OpenAI announced visual display ads that appear alongside image-generation results in ChatGPT. They begin later this month in the U.S. only, with a first group of advertisers. OpenAI says the ads will be labeled and will not influence the answers, and it added attribution partners such as AppsFlyer and Northbeam so advertisers can measure results.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: ChatGPT reaches about 1.2 billion weekly users, so a new ad channel is coming. Watch it, but test with a small budget before you commit.
Source: TechCrunch

Gemini

Starting October 9, free Gemini users lose Flash and Pro and keep only Flash-Lite. AI Plus ($4.99/month) loses Pro access, while AI Pro ($19.99/month) gains Deep Think reasoning, and the app adds low, medium and high effort settings. Google's new Gemini 4 Argon model remains limited to select cybersecurity partners.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If your team relies on free Gemini for real work, expect weaker answers on Friday. Decide whether the $19.99 plan is worth it, or move that task elsewhere.
Source: AI Weekly

Claude

Announced October 6 at SF Tech Week, Anthropic expanded Claude for Startups: one free year of Claude Team with up to five seats, $1,000 in API credits and office hours. Companies qualify if they were founded within five years or raised funding within two.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If your business is under five years old, apply before you pay for any AI team plan. Free seats are the cheapest way to test it with your staff.
Source: TechCrunch

Manus

Quiet week. The big news was the Manus 2.0 and Cue personal-agent launch at the end of September, which we covered last issue.

Source: Bloomberg

Microsoft

Microsoft is restructuring Microsoft 365 Copilot billing into a flat per-user license for everyday AI plus usage-based Copilot Credits for advanced features. Per the report, nothing is charged until an admin creates a spending policy, and OneDrive pay-as-you-go storage arrives in November.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: If you manage Microsoft 365, set a spending policy in the admin center now so usage-based charges cannot surprise you.
Source: ChangePilot

Grok

Quiet week. The latest items are small: xAI retired an older speech-to-text model on October 2 in favor of grok-voice-transcribe-2.0 at the same price, after Team Bots and Grok 4.7 in late September.

Source: xAI release notes (via Releasebot)
INTERNATIONAL

🇫🇷 France

Mistral unveiled Mistral Large 4, nicknamed 'Le Chonk,' a 1.05-trillion-parameter multimodal model. It is in public preview now, with open weights planned in about three weeks after safety testing. Mistral says it trained on roughly 4,000 Nvidia GPUs, which it describes as far fewer than rivals.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Open-weight models can be run privately, which matters if you handle client data. Keep an eye on this when the weights are released.
Source: TechCrunch

🇪🇺 European Union

Quiet week. The most recent rule change is the AI Omnibus, in force since July 27, which pushes high-risk (Annex III) deadlines to December 2, 2027 and extends simplified obligations to small mid-cap companies.

Source: European Commission

🇨🇳 China

Bloomberg-sourced reports say DeepSeek is nearing a funding round of at least 80 billion yuan (about $12 billion), led by Tencent and CATL, well above its original 50 billion yuan target. The previous round valued it at $52-59 billion.

WHY IT MATTERS FOR SMBs: Heavy investment keeps price pressure on every AI vendor. Expect good-enough models to keep getting cheaper, which helps your budget.
Source: The Next Web

PRACTICAL TAKEAWAY

This week, make a one-page list of every AI tool your business uses and mark each one free, paid or ad-supported. Then check which ones lose features or change pricing this month (Gemini free tier on October 9 and Microsoft Copilot billing are two). Pick one paid tool you truly rely on and budget for it; drop the rest.
